How this radar prices a launch

Every protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is DefiLlama's record, not proof: a protocol may carry a token its record does not show. The board's implied value is this radar's own claim about one question — what would a token launch be worth if the protocol's record stayed exactly as it is? It is not a prediction that any of these protocols will issue a token, and no figure here comes from a hand-written list of names: the same arithmetic prices every row.

Step 1 — what the market already pays for a dollar of TVL

The snapshot's tokenised protocols carry both a market cap and a TVL, so each category has an observed market-cap-to-TVL multiple. A category is priced at its median observed multiple, winsorised at the snapshot's own 90th percentile (so one outlier — a prediction market trading at 400× its TVL — cannot set a whole category's price) and shrunk toward the global median in proportion to the evidence: multiple = (n × median + 8 × global median) / (n + 8), where n is the category's tokenised count. A category with no tokenised protocol takes the global median. The table below is computed from the snapshot at run time, not typed in.

Step 2 — four adjustments from the protocol's own record

  • Momentum — 1 + 0.6 × clamp(7-day TVL change, −30%, +30%) / 100. A week of growth or flight, damped.
  • Maturity — 0.78 + 0.30 × min(age, 4 years) / 4 years, where age runs from the day DefiLlama began tracking the protocol to the snapshot day. Longer running with no token, more credible the launch. An unrecorded age takes 0.92.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, up to ten: a launch is worth more where more users already are.
  • Size — (TVL / $100M)−0.18, clamped to [0.65, 1.30]. In the snapshot itself, tokenised protocols trade at a median 0.65× TVL in the smallest TVL tercile, 0.32× in the middle and 0.08× in the largest: big TVL is worth less per dollar, and the model says so.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth × size

Who is ranked — and who is not

Above the snapshot's $50M watchlist floor, the board carries every one of the 40 watchlist protocols and treats that list as complete: a large protocol the house left off was left off for a reason the snapshot does not show (DefiLlama naming a parent protocol), so the radar does not second-guess it. Below the floor it applies the house's published screen itself — no exchanges, no bridges or staking wrappers of tokens that already trade, no versioned pools, no wrapped or staked product names — and ranks everything that passes. Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked; the largest are shown apart, at the market's own values.

Reading a farm plan

Each row's plan is built from the protocol's category, chains, TVL and age: the position a farmer would actually take, what it costs in capital and gas, and how long it runs. Capital is sized to the protocol ($50–$250), hold windows lengthen for younger protocols, and every plan repeats one safety rule in its own words: claim only from links the protocol itself publishes.
